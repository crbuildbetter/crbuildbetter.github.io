AI / Data Science Engineer

Open AI Data Science

buildbetter is hiring a highly capable, versatile AI / Data Science Engineer to own the full lifecycle of data-driven systems, from raw data pipelines to putting intelligent models into production.

View job details

About us

buildbetter is a partner company of ProGaming and specializes in software development, AI applications, and AI solutions of many kinds. We are looking for a highly capable, versatile AI / Data Science Engineer to join the team.

In this role, you will own the lifecycle of data-driven systems from end to end. You won't just build models in theory: you will build raw data pipelines and push intelligent models into production. If you enjoy the challenge of designing complex architectures and deploying AI applications in the real world, we'd love to work with you!

Responsibilities

  • Data Collection: Design, build, and maintain scalable, automated data collection pipelines from sources such as databases, APIs, system logs, and third-party integrations.
  • Data Cleansing & Preparation: Design preprocessing, parsing, and normalization processes, perform feature engineering, and handle missing or inconsistent data to create high-quality datasets.
  • Modeling: Select, design, and train the machine learning, deep learning, or statistical models best suited to business problems and application features.
  • Evaluation: Build rigorous evaluation frameworks and validate models against benchmarks for performance, accuracy, scalability, and bias before release.
  • Deployment: Containerize, deploy, and scale AI models in production through microservices, REST/GraphQL APIs, and MLOps tools, ensuring smooth integration with our applications and games.
  • Monitoring & Optimization: Continuously monitor model drift and production performance, and set up automated retraining loops and optimization cycles.
  • Collaboration: Work with software developers, product managers, and stakeholders to turn business goals into practical technical solutions.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, data science, statistics, mathematics, or a related quantitative field.
  • Hands-on experience across the full lifecycle of data pipeline management and machine learning development.
  • Expertise in Python and its ecosystem (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn, SciPy).
  • Deep understanding of major ML/DL frameworks (e.g. TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras).
  • Strong skills in SQL and database design (relational and NoSQL architectures).
  • Experience with containerization and cloud architecture (Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, or Azure) for serving models at scale.
  • Familiarity with MLOps concepts and CI/CD pipelines for machine learning is a plus.
  • A strong problem-solving mindset, analytical skills, and a proactive, collaborative attitude.
